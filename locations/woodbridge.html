Why is the lower level of my Woodbridge split level always cold?

Lower levels sit below grade and lose heat to cooler ground, so they feel a weak blower or leaking duct first. We measure temperatures on each level, test blower output and inspect accessible ducts. Many cases improve with an airflow correction or a blower repair rather than a new furnace.

What does it mean when my furnace blows cool air in Woodbridge?

Cool air usually means the fan is running but the burners are not lit, often due to a failed igniter, a dirty flame sensor or a safety lockout. Turn the thermostat up and note any clicking or error light, then call (703) 962-7410 so we can test the ignition sequence safely.

How do I know if a heat exchanger problem is serious?

A cracked or heavily corroded heat exchanger can let combustion gases mix with household air, so it is always treated as a safety issue. We inspect the exchanger on older Woodbridge furnaces, explain any evidence we find and will not run a furnace we believe is unsafe.

Does living near the Occoquan River affect my furnace?

River air adds dampness and wind exposure, which can mean more frost at vent terminations and longer furnace run times during storms. We check vents and condensate drains carefully for homes near Belmont Bay and the Occoquan, and confirm the furnace can sustain long cycles without shutting down.
