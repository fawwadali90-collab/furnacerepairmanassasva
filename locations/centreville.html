Townhouses in Sully Station and Centre Ridge have compact duct runs, so a weak blower, clogged filter or closed damper affects one floor quickly. We check airflow at each register, inspect accessible ducts and test the blower to find where the balance is being lost.

Can a dirty flame sensor cause my furnace to shut off?

Yes. A fouled flame sensor can make the furnace light, sense no flame and shut down for safety, then repeat the cycle. Cleaning or replacing the sensor often restores steady heat. We also check why it fouled, since weak airflow or a burner issue can cause it to happen again.
